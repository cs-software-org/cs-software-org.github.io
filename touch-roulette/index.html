Touch Roulette.

Touch Roulette is a free random finger picker for Android — a quick, fair way to decide who goes first. Everyone places a finger on the screen, someone taps Start, and the roulette picks one person.

01

How it works

  1. Fingers down

    Everyone who’s playing places one finger on the screen. Each finger gets its own coloured circle.

  2. Tap Start

    When everyone is ready, tap Start Roulette and let the wheel spin.

  3. One is picked

    The roulette selects a finger at random. Lift your fingers and tap Reset to play again.

02

Four ways to play

  • Classic

    Randomly selects one person from the group.

  • Team

    Splits everyone into two random teams, Red vs Blue.

  • Pairs

    Pairs everyone up with a random partner.

  • Ranked

    Ranks every player from first to last.

Team and Pairs need an even number of fingers.

Common questions

What is Touch Roulette?

Touch Roulette is a free random finger picker and decision maker for Android, made by CS Software. Everyone places a finger on the screen and the app randomly picks one person. It has been downloaded more than a million times on Google Play.

How does Touch Roulette work?

Everyone who is playing places one finger on the screen. When everyone is ready, tap Start Roulette. The roulette spins and randomly selects one finger. Lift your fingers and tap Reset to play again.

Is Touch Roulette free? Does it have ads?

Touch Roulette is free to download on Google Play and contains ads. There is no subscription and nothing to sign up for.

Does Touch Roulette collect personal data?

No. Touch Roulette’s Google Play Data safety section states that the app collects no data and shares no data with third parties. See our Privacy Policy.

Is Touch Roulette suitable for kids and classrooms?

Touch Roulette is rated Everyone on Google Play. It works well for picking a student to answer in class, taking turns at family game night, or deciding who does the chores.

Why do Team and Pairs modes need an even number of fingers?

So that everyone can be split evenly: Team mode makes two equal teams, and Pairs mode gives every player a partner.

Is Touch Roulette available on iPhone?

Not yet. Touch Roulette is available for Android on Google Play, and an iPhone version is in the works.
