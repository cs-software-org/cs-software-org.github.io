Common questions

What is CS Software?

CS Software is an independent, one-person studio that makes simple, focused mobile apps. Its first app, Touch Roulette, has been downloaded more than a million times on Google Play.

What apps does CS Software make?

CS Software’s first app is Touch Roulette, a random group picker and decision maker for Android. Everyone places a finger on the screen and the app picks one person at random.

Are CS Software’s apps free?

Yes. Touch Roulette is free to download on Google Play and is supported by ads. There are no subscriptions and nothing to sign up for.

Do CS Software’s apps collect personal data?

No. Our apps are designed to work without collecting personal information. Touch Roulette’s Google Play Data safety section states that no data is collected and no data is shared with third parties. See our Privacy Policy.
